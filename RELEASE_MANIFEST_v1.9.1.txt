AI-TRADER v1.9.1

CORE CHANGE
- Final verified confidence is the sole strategy-level entry authority.
- Once final confidence >= configured minimum, execution path submits immediately without a second strategy/risk/news/MTF gate.
- Existing active thesis/position identity prevents duplicate opens.

EXECUTION TRUTH
- Broker/terminal failures remain physical execution failures (e.g. disconnected MT5, disabled AutoTrading, broker permissions, missing SL/TP, broker rejection). The application cannot force a broker fill.
- Such failures are reported as EXECUTION_FAILED, not as a strategy confidence block.

CHART/UI
- Existing advanced analysis retained.
- Added Classic Gold, Institutional, Carbon and TradingView Dark global themes.

STABILITY
- run_bridge.bat now detects an existing listener on 127.0.0.1:18812 and exits without spawning a duplicate bridge.
